Every page has the same structure: breadcrumb → H1 → intro → image block (existing) → What's included (cards/list) → How we deliver (steps) → Scope and boundaries → Related → FAQs (accordion) → CTA.

Standard **How we deliver** steps (reuse on every page and edit the verbs where needed):
1. **Discover:** Understand goals, users, current systems and constraints.
2. **Scope:** Agree deliverables, integrations, timeline and responsibilities in writing.
3. **Build and test:** Deliver in reviewable stages, tested against agreed examples.
4. **Launch and support:** Train users, hand over documentation and support to the agreed plan.

Standard **FAQs** (use on every page, plus the page-specific ones):
- **Can you work with our existing software?** We assess your systems, available APIs and access permissions before confirming integration scope.
- **Can we start with a small project?** Yes. A defined pilot or first module lets us validate requirements and the approach before a wider rollout.
- **How long will implementation take?** It depends on scope, integrations, data quality and review cycles. You receive a delivery plan after discovery.
- **Do you provide support after launch?** Support and maintenance can be included in the agreed scope, with coverage and response expectations documented in the proposal.

Standard pricing line: *Pricing depends on modules, users, customisation, integrations and deployment requirements. Share your requirements for a scoped proposal.*
