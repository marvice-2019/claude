## What is generative engine optimization?

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the practice of making a brand easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Microsoft Copilot to find, trust and quote. Traditional SEO gets a page into Google's list of links. GEO gets your business named inside the AI-written answer, often the only thing the searcher reads.

## Why it matters now

- AI answers now sit above the organic results for a growing share of commercial searches, and many of those searches end without a click.
- AI assistants pick a handful of brands per answer. If your name, services and location aren't clearly and consistently described across the web, you aren't one of them.
- Most Indian SMEs haven't started, so the brands that move first set the default answer in their category.

## GEO vs SEO: do I need both?

Yes. GEO builds on SEO rather than replacing it. AI Overviews and ChatGPT search draw heavily from pages that already rank and are well structured. Marvice runs both together, so the same work lifts your Google rankings and your AI citations.
