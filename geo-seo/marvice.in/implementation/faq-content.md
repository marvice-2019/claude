## What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

Generative engine optimization is the practice of making a brand easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude to find, understand and cite. It combines structured data, entity consistency across the web, citable answer-style content, llms.txt and AI-crawler access. Marvice Media runs GEO alongside traditional SEO so a business ranks on Google and is named in AI answers.

## How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO aims to rank a page in Google's list of links. GEO aims to get a brand mentioned or cited inside an AI-generated answer, where there is often no list to click. Both rely on crawlable, trustworthy content, but GEO puts extra weight on clear entity data (schema, consistent name-address-phone), third-party mentions and short factual passages that an AI can quote.

## How long does SEO or GEO take to show results?

Technical fixes such as schema, indexing clean-up and AI-crawler access are usually picked up within 2–6 weeks. Local rankings in Bengaluru typically move within 2–4 months. Competitive national keywords and consistent AI citations usually take 4–9 months of content and authority building.
