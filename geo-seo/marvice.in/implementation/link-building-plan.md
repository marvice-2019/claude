## Week 3–6: Earned links (~15 links)

1. **Client credit links** — ask 10 clients (Hyundai/Yamaha dealers, BIFA, Beyond Conceptz, BlockMyAdmission, etc.) for a footer credit "Website/Marketing by Marvice Media" or a testimonial on their site linking to the relevant service page. Highest-relevance links you can get.
2. **Case studies on Clutch/GoodFirms** — get 5 verified client reviews each; these listings rank and get cited.
3. **Associations** — NASSCOM, TiE Bangalore/Chennai, BNI chapter, local chambers (Bangalore Chamber of Industry & Commerce, MCCI Chennai): member profile pages link out.
4. **College placement / guest-lecture pages** — offer a GEO/digital marketing session to 3 MBA colleges in Bengaluru/Chennai; they list speakers with links.
5. **Event pages** — every Conxyou event: ask the venue/partner to credit "Event by Conxyou (Marvice Media)".

## Week 7–12: Authority & AI citations (~10 links)

1. **"Best agencies" listicles** — search `best digital marketing agencies in Bengaluru`, `best GEO agency Bangalore`, `top branding agency Chennai`; email each author with the pitch below. AI assistants lean on these lists.
2. **Expert quotes** — sign up on Qwoted and Featured.com; answer 3 queries/week on SEO, GEO, AI search.
3. **Guest articles** — pitch YourStory, Inc42, Entrackr, Social Samosa, afaqs!, exchange4media: "How Indian SMBs can get recommended by ChatGPT" (repurpose the GEO blog posts).
4. **Data asset** — publish "AI Search Visibility of 50 Bengaluru businesses" (run the GEO audit on 50 local brands); pitch to local press. Original data earns links on its own.

## Avoid

Paid link packages, PBNs, bulk "500 directory" gigs, comment/forum spam, and link exchanges — they risk a Google penalty and add nothing for AI citations.
