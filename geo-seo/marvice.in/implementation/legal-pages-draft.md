## Privacy Policy (page 2441)

This Privacy Policy explains how Marvice Media Private Limited ("Marvice Media", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you visit marvice.in, contact us or use our services, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

### Information We Collect

We collect only the personal data needed to respond to you and deliver our services. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

When you fill in our contact or job application forms, email us, call us or become a client, we collect details such as your name, company, email address, phone number, the content of your message and, for clients, billing and GST details.

We collect technical information automatically using the following technologies:

- Cookies: small files that keep the site working and remember your preferences.
- Server log files: IP address, browser type, pages visited and time of visit, used for security and performance.
- Analytics tools: aggregated statistics on how visitors use the site, used to improve our content.


You can disable cookies in your browser settings; some parts of the site may not work as intended if you do.

We use personal data to respond to enquiries, prepare proposals, deliver and invoice services, send updates you have asked for, improve our website and meet legal and tax obligations.

We process your data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you submit a form or contact us, or where the processing is needed to perform a contract with you or comply with the law. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to us.

### Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only where necessary with:

- Service providers who help us run our business, such as hosting, email, analytics and payment providers, under confidentiality obligations.
- Professional advisers such as auditors and lawyers.
- Government or regulatory authorities where required by law.


### Data Retention and Security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law (for example, tax records), and then delete or anonymise it. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect it.

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you have the right to access a summary of your personal data, request correction or erasure, withdraw consent, nominate another person to exercise your rights, and raise a grievance with us.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@marvice.in with the subject "Data Request". We will respond within the timelines required by law. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
